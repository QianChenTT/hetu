The router: the one path every call and event takes and the enforcement point on it. It binds the caller from the way in, applies the floor, asks the gate policy job and routes each call to the server of its namespace, found through the contract's namespace table (`namespaces` in `core/api/ids.ts`): a provider's call to the core module serving it, a core action to the part handed in under the table's name for it, the bus's actions to the bus and anything else to the plugin of that id. It names no action and no part but those on the list in `core/test/router-names.test.ts`, each with why. It decides no grant itself. Plain Node, no Electron. Security requests and the window lifecycle actions are core actions the floor decides and never the gate, so a failed gate can't block the way out

The steps import downward only, `open-calls.ts` first, then `provide.ts`, `shape.ts` and `route.ts`, `door.ts` and the class:

- `router.ts`: the `Router` class joining the steps and its door: `register`, `end`, `send`, `limit`, `stopping`, `shutting`, `changed`, `revokeAll`, `status`, `quitting`, `publishCore` and what the plugins manager and the runtime hand it: the plugin table (`read`), each part serving a namespace (`serve(part, served)`), the prompt behind an ask (`asks`) and the listeners for a closed way and for what a half sent (`onWayClosed`, `onHeard`). `changed()` resyncs the bus and, on a new grants snapshot, has the gate judge every open call with power again
- `context.ts`: what the steps share: a way in, an open call (`Pending`), the readers of the plugin table that turn a throw into a closed answer and the `Context` holding the options and the ways. `pairOf` gives the pair a chain carries, keyed by the grant key of its root's onBehalf, while the call keeps the whole onBehalf for its record
- `door.ts`: the ways in (ports, host handles, the window seam with its sender check first), each message from a utility half told as heard before the rate counts it, the rate and the flood cap from flood control, a way that closes told to the listeners before the router answers the calls its plugin held
- `shape.ts`: 2 shape and 3 floor. The strict envelope, the contract's schema for the action the namespace table routes to, a `parent` that names a live call on the same way, its `onBehalf` taken by every call under it, then who may call what: the floor for core actions by their `from`, a plugin's host half in its utility process for `utility` and the plugin the root of a chain called under that call for `pair`, the trusted window's actions inside its input delay refused but the reads the contract marks and the floor for `events.*`. Whatever the gate's state a path with a dot segment answers `invalid` here, since the contract's path schema refuses it for every action
- `route.ts`: the entry of every call through its steps and 6 route: a part handed in under its name, the bus, a provider or a plugin's declared action. An action that names a live call (`gate.filterReadable`) goes on only for a call delivered on the asking callee's own way. The part gets a view of it (`LiveCall`). A callee's answer matches only a call delivered on its own way
- `provide.ts`: 5 precheck to 10 perform. The gate's answers on the current grants snapshot, each for the caller and, for a call under the root call of a chain under a plugin root, the pair that chain carries, the guards in order with a deny from the lock guard answered `locked`, a provider's prepare, the latch it holds until perform or release, the frozen copy perform acts on, the judge a call with power carries to its provider for a second ask, a listing filtered through `canList` with its gate time charged to the gate module (a charge that throws recorded as a fault) and refused `policy-down` with a fault when that count throws or answers other than yes or no, a stored key that fails its hash recorded as `<part>.tampered` and `rejudge`, which asks the gate again about every open call it let on once a new grants snapshot applied and `revokeAll` for when it couldn't
- `open-calls.ts`: 4 ceiling and the life of an open call: flood control's room, the deadline shrinking along `parent`, `cancel`, `stop`, `finish`, the one exit of every call where 11 record happens and the call's signal aborts. `revoke` is for a call a new grants snapshot no longer covers and every call under it: a plugin callee is cancelled and its caller answered `stale`, as is a call not yet in perform, a provider in perform hears the signal and the call answers `stale` once its perform ends, thrown or not, so a single write that finishes lands and its result is dropped. Its second ask (`judgeAgain`) answers the revoke's code. A call under it still in perform is no longer its sub-call, so the call answering doesn't stop it
- `bus.ts`: subscriptions, credits and delivery, below
- `limits.ts`: the limits as they apply to one plugin with the human's values
- `header.ts`: its door and what the later parts give it, each a small interface the tests fake: the gate, the providers and flood control from the core modules, recording, the guards, which nothing holds yet and the gate module's count of the gate's time (`gateTime`)

Who writes an open call (`Pending`, D-632): `open-calls.ts` makes it in `admitted` and what is set there stays as set. Each field written after that has one owner, which writes it through a small setter of its own:

| Owner | Fields |
|---|---|
| `open-calls.ts` | `timer`, `children` (changed only in place), `done`, `revoked`, `live` |
| `provide.ts` | `gate`, `scope`, `refusedScope`, `judged`, `performing` |
| `route.ts` | `callee`, `coalesce` |

Every field of `Pending` is `readonly`, so `npm run typecheck` fails any other write whatever the variable is called. `readonly` and the cast check cover typed writes only: `Object.assign`, `as any` or a loosely typed alias get past both and only review catches them. The setters stay unexported but `setScope`, which `route.ts` calls with the topics a subscribe made live. A write outside the owner that has to stay casts to `Writable`, which drops only `readonly`, with its why on the line: `(pending as Writable<Pending>).scope = x // clash: <why>`. `core/test/clash-casts.test.ts` fails a cast with no why or a why under 10 characters. It holds the list of every clash, none today, so a new one fails until it is listed and a growing list says the rule needs updating. It also fails a setter called from any shipped file of core off its list. The way in (`Way`) is not a call and is left out

The parts it is handed: the plugins manager serves `core.*`, `security.*` and `plugin.*`, the runtime `app.*` and `editor.*`, recording `log.*`, the gate module `gate.*` and the drawer `ui.*`, each through `serve` under the part's name in the table. A namespace whose part nobody handed in answers `unavailable`. Recording also takes each call's record and the router's own core records. The log's state decides nothing of a call. A throw in a part is that call failed or that record lost

Every call carries a signal into perform (`Call.signal`), which aborts when the call ends. On a new grants snapshot the router asks the gate again, with no prompt, about every open call with power on the frozen copy it judged. A refused one is revoked as `stale`, or as `policy-down` when the gate throws or is down. A call not yet judged is judged on the newest grants snapshot when it gets there and a widened grant stops nothing. With the gate off by the human every call passes and no open call is judged again. When the plugins manager's `changed()` throws it records a fault and calls `revokeAll` (D-646): nothing was judged again, so every open call with power a plugin holds is revoked as `policy-down` the same way, whether or not its grant changed. A call whose end throws is recorded as a fault `router/revokeAll` and the rest still end. Every live subscription of a plugin the gate holds ends with `events.ended` and the next sync asks again about every one that waits. A window's grants are fixed, so its open calls with power end only when the gate isn't on, answered as `rejudge` answers them then: `policy-down` with the gate failed or absent, `stale` while it starts (D-649). With the gate on they run on. A window's subscriptions are left to the next sync, as `rejudge` leaves them, and anything with no power runs on. With the gate off nothing ends

The bus, in the router:
- `events.subscribe`, `events.unsubscribe` and `events.publish` are calls and take the eleven steps. The `event` and `ack` messages and the signals are shaped in `core/api/envelope.ts`
- a plugin publishes only the `<id>.<name>` topics its manifest declared, from any of its halves. The luoshu, a core module, publishes `luoshu.changed` and `luoshu.outside` and the lock module `lock.changed` through `publishCore`, which delivers as a publish does and makes no audit record of a call. A topic not in the contract's `moduleTopics`, or with fields other than its own, reaches nobody. It hears its own for free. `core.status`, `core.security` and `core.quitting` come only from the router and go only to windows, with no path. The trusted window hears nothing else. These floor rules hold with the gate off
- one subscription per half and topic, so a repeat replaces it, at most `topicsPerHalf` per half. A subscribe to another plugin's topics is judged at both stages under `events.subscribe` scoped by topics. One made while the gate can't be asked (failed, on with no holder or with the grants snapshot unreadable) stands until the gate allows it
- a path goes out only under the gate's `canList`, or `canRead` for a topic `moduleTopics` gives to readers alone (`lock.changed`), every path with the gate off by the human, none while the gate can't be asked. Path-less events go out while the gate is down to the subscriptions it allowed on the current grants snapshot
- a subscription the gate holds hears anything only while the gate allowed it on the current grants snapshot or the gate is off by the human. So a grants snapshot that moves while the gate is down holds back what the gate allowed on the old one. What was taken with the gate off waits when the gate is switched back on and can't be asked
- when the gate's state or the grants snapshot moves the bus asks the gate again about every subscription it holds. A refused one ends with `events.ended`. One the gate gives no answer about, a throw or an answer on an older grants snapshot included, is held back and asked about again at every sync until the gate answers. One whose item now asks at first use, as when another plugin's change moves its plugin into class 4, is in use: the ask raises the item's Confirm for the human and the subscription is held back the same way, with nothing delivered, until the Confirm lets it start or a declined Confirm or a refusal ends it. A path subscriber that the gate went down under, or a held back one that starts, gets one `events.dropped` once the gate is up, so it reads again
- at most `eventCredits` events in flight per half until its `ack`. Past them events are dropped and counted, nothing more goes out until the half has acknowledged all it was sent and then one `events.dropped` names what it missed. Signals take no credit
- a way that ends takes its subscriptions and credits with it, so a new incarnation starts with none
- records: each subscribe, unsubscribe and publish is a call with its audit record, publishes and free calls coalesced per caller per second. A subscribe whose gated topics all stand used no power, so its record names only the topics that went live and coalesces like a free call. Drops are counted in coalesced `events.dropped` core records. What stands goes in a coalesced `events.waiting` record and what asking again ended, started or held back in one `events.ended`, `events.started` or `events.waiting` record per subscriber per change, each with its topics. Deliveries and acks record nothing and recording off stops all of it

It imports nothing from `boot`
