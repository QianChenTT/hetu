Flood control, a core module in main that bounds what plugins and windows may cost main. It counts and says yes or no while the carrier acts on its answer

- `flood-control.ts`: `createFloodControl`, made by `core/backend/wire.ts` and held by `core/backend/modules/modules.ts`. `admit` counts each way's messages against its rate and its flood cap before the carrier reads them (`pass`, `drop` or `flood`, which closes a plugin's way and never a window's). `acquire` charges a call's bytes to the caller and to the root of its chain once the call parsed, null past either one's calls or bytes in flight. `release` gives them back from the carrier's `finish`, the one exit of every call. The numbers come from the limits it is handed, the human's for one plugin or for all, a window never below the fixed values. `pacedLimit` is how the relay in the worker host page, a utility half's own transport and main's end of a utility half's port pace a plugin's messages: by the limits while flood control runs and not at all while the human has it off
- `header.ts`: its door, the shapes the carrier and the holder read

Switched off, the holder answers null and the carrier counts nothing, drops nothing, closes nothing and throttles nothing, for plugins and windows alike. Every running plugin is handed its limits again so nothing paces its messages. Switching it off is a security control's switch, held for the trusted window's Confirm and typed. A throw in `admit` drops that one message, one in `acquire` answers that call `failed` and one in `release` is recorded, each as a `fault`. It runs on through shutdown and the stopped mode. It imports no Node built-in. Its tests are in `core/test/flood-control.test.ts` and the carrier's path tests in `core/test/carrier-limits.test.ts`

Roles: `flood-control.ts` policy holder, since it says yes or no and the carrier enforces, `header.ts` contract
